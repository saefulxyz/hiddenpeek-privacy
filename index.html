Privacy Policy for HiddenPeek

Effective date: 2026-11-25

HiddenPeek ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the HiddenPeek mobile application.

Information We Collect

HiddenPeek itself does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, address, or phone number.

The app may store game progress locally on your device, such as coins, unlocked levels, and audio settings.

Third-Party Services

HiddenPeek uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or your device, including:

  • Google AdMob - for displaying rewarded advertisements.
  • Google Play services - for normal Android app functionality where applicable.

These services may collect data such as device identifiers, IP address, ad interaction data, diagnostics, and approximate location depending on your settings and applicable consent. Please review Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Policies

Ads

HiddenPeek displays rewarded ads. You can choose whether to watch rewarded ads to receive in-game coins. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

Children's Privacy

HiddenPeek is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party services used by the app, please contact us.

Data Security

We value your trust and strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting information. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
